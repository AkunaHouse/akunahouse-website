How we deliver

The same four stages on every contract, so the contracting officer and COR always know where the event stands.

  1. Quote

    Every requirement in the solicitation answered, priced by CLIN with an extended total, submitted early and receipt confirmed.

  2. Source

    Venue, caterer and AV secured in writing from local partners, with date holds and per diem pricing locked.

  3. Run

    Pre-performance meeting, setup and walkthrough the day before, owner on site from first setup to final breakdown.

  4. Close

    Headcount and meal records to the COR, invoice in WAWF after acceptance, partners paid promptly.
