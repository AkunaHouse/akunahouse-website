Our specialties

One accountable prime for the whole event.

For National Guard and Reserve units, federal agencies, state offices and corporate teams. Each job is a single firm-fixed-price contract with one point of contact from quote to close-out.

Yellow Ribbon & family readiness events

Pre-deployment, post-deployment and reintegration events: divisible general-session space, buffet meals at per diem, childcare rooms, vendor tables and parking.

Government lodging

Room blocks for units, work crews, travel and events, at or under the local GSA per diem — one contract, one invoice.

Conferences, training & career fairs

Agency conferences, industry days, training events and career fairs, from 50 to 1,000+ attendees, with AV and an on-site technician.

Chaplain, resiliency & family retreats

One- and two-night retreats for service members and families: lodging, meals, meeting space and programme logistics.

Meeting & venue rental

Conference rooms, banquet space and meeting facilities sourced, contracted and set to your requirements.

State & agency meetings

Board meetings, workshops and multi-day sessions for state offices and agencies, managed end to end.
