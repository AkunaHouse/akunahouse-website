About Akuna

Built by someone who's actually run the business — not just advised one.

Akuna is one brand with two pillars, Business and Body, and one conviction: a thriving business and a thriving owner go hand in hand.

The name

Akuna means "to flow".

Our name draws from the Dieri language of Australia, and we acknowledge the Dieri people as its Traditional Custodians. The Akuna Flow Mark evokes water, movement and gathering: dots grow bold and full on one side, then trail and fade as they move — energy in motion from a still centre.

Our aim is to bring you back to that same still centre.
